# Framework — The Five Pillars (Kokoro)

**Phase:** M1–2 (Foundation) — Framework v1 drafted; published M3–4
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

Define the five-pillar wisdom methodology that underlies the Kokoro project. The pillars are not software features — they are a framework of practices, principles, and measurable commitments that guide how the pilot is run and how the organisation relates to its people, its environment, and its communities. The framework outlives the engine. It is the methodology that travels when the technology fades.

Each pillar is grounded in a concept drawn from Japanese and Vietnamese cultural-religious tradition. Together they form an integrated framework for cross-cultural, dignity-first, sustainability-oriented work.

## The Five Pillars

### Pillar I — 心 Kokoro: Cultural-Religious Literacy

**Principle:** Know the places you touch.

Religion and culture are wisdom sources, not compliance risks. Before acting in any cultural context, learn the moral grammar of that context.

**Core practices:**
- Cultural-religious literacy programme for all staff
- Paid local cultural-religious advisor per host country
- Religious-calendar-aware operations (major observances respected operationally)
- Pre-launch cultural review of all products and campaigns

**KPIs:**
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Literacy programme completion | ≥95% within 12 months of joining |
| Local advisors in place | ≥1 per host country |
| Major religious observances respected | 100% |
| Products/campaigns with pre-launch cultural review | 100% |
| Cultural missteps per 100k stakeholder interactions | <2 |

---

### Pillar II — 命 Inochi: Stewardship of Nature

**Principle:** Treat the Earth as ancestor, not asset. Land, water, forest, and climate are borrowed from future generations.

**Core practices:**
- Emissions trajectory aligned to 1.5°C (SBTi-validated)
- Biodiversity net-gain tracking on operational footprint
- Water stewardship to AWS standard
- Free, Prior, Informed Consent (FPIC) for any operations adjacent to indigenous communities
- Circular materials procurement

**KPIs:**
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Emissions trajectory | 1.5°C-aligned, SBTi-validated; –50% by 2030 |
| Biodiversity net-gain | ≥+10% on operational footprint by 2030 |
| Water balance | 100% operational sites water-balanced (AWS standard) by 2030 |
| FPIC compliance | 100% for indigenous-adjacent operations |
| Renewable / recycled material inputs | ≥80% by 2030 |

---

### Pillar III — 心 Tâm: Dignity of People

**Principle:** Dignity is owed, not earned. It is a precondition of any relationship, not a reward for performance.

**Core practices:**
- Living wage for all direct staff and tier-1 suppliers
- Annual modern-slavery supply chain audit
- Psychological safety measurement
- Religious accommodation with defined response time
- Inclusive design testing with diverse cultural-religious user groups

**KPIs:**
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Living wage coverage | 100% direct staff + tier-1 suppliers by 2028 |
| Modern-slavery audit coverage | 100% tier-1 suppliers audited annually |
| Employee dignity index | ≥80th percentile |
| Median religious accommodation resolution time | <48 hours |
| Cultural-religious user groups per product tested | ≥3 |

---

### Pillar IV — 縁 En: Service to Community

**Principle:** Bound, not contracted. Communities are owed long-term commitment — not project-cycle attention.

**Core practices:**
- Local hiring preference in every host market
- Revenue reinvestment in host communities
- Active cultural heritage partnership per host country
- Pre-committed crisis-response budget
- Annual community council with binding input

**KPIs:**
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Local hiring rate | ≥60% of workforce per host country |
| Revenue reinvested in host markets | ≥1% of revenue |
| Active heritage partnerships | ≥1 per host country |
| Community council held | Annual, every host market |
| Crisis-response budget committed | Pre-committed regional budget in place |

---

### Pillar V — 誠 Makoto: Transparency & Accountability

**Principle:** Sincerity made checkable. It is not enough to assert good intentions — they must be audited and verifiable.

**Core practices:**
- Integrated annual reporting (financial + ESG + cultural)
- Third-party assurance of all Kokoro KPIs
- Public Kokoro dashboard (live, quarterly refresh)
- Board-level governance of the framework
- Anonymous multilingual whistleblower channel

**KPIs:**
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Integrated reporting | Annual, GRI + IFRS S2 aligned |
| KPIs with third-party assurance | 100% |
| Public dashboard refresh cadence | Quarterly |
| Board meeting time on Kokoro | ≥20% |
| Whistleblower response time | <72 hours |

## Functional Requirements

**FR-FRM-001:** Framework v1 must be drafted and reviewed by the cultural advisor and Shizenkan advisor before M3.

**FR-FRM-002:** The framework must be published as a standalone playbook (40–60 pages) by M4, covering all five pillars with their principles, practices, and KPIs.

**FR-FRM-003:** The framework vocabulary must be treated as heuristic — a navigational tool — not as a fixed typology. The cultural advisor must co-author all pillar definitions to prevent cultural essentialism.

**FR-FRM-004:** Each pillar must have at least one practice that is active during the pilot (not deferred to post-pilot). The pilot is the first implementation of the framework, not a rehearsal.

**FR-FRM-005:** The framework must be measurable. Every KPI must have a defined baseline measurement method before it can be reported.

**FR-FRM-006:** The framework must be separable from the engine — it must be applicable by organisations that do not use the AI engine.

**FR-FRM-007:** A public version of the playbook (20 pages, non-confidential) must be prepared by M8 for open release to Asian cross-border organisations.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Framework v1 document exists and has been reviewed by both advisors before M3 build begins
- Every KPI has a defined measurement method and a named owner
- The pilot implements at least one practice per pillar during the 8-month duration
- The 40–60 page playbook is complete and reviewed by M4
- The 20-page public playbook is ready for release by M8

---

## Constraints

- Pillar vocabulary must be validated with the cultural advisor — no term from Japanese or Vietnamese tradition is used without advisory confirmation of accuracy and appropriateness
- KPI targets must be set in collaboration with Vnext leadership; they cannot be set unilaterally by the project lead
- The framework must not be positioned as a compliance checklist — its purpose is conscience-driven action, not box-ticking

---

## Out of Scope (Pilot Phase)

- Formal GRI / IFRS S2 reporting (Horizon 2 / post-pilot)
- Third-party assurance certification (Horizon 2)
- Community councils in markets beyond the pilot scope
- Net-positive Inochi targets (Horizon 3)
